About Dr. Blanco

Hi, I'm Jacqueline. I'm glad you're here.

Reaching out can feel like a big step, and it's completely normal to feel a little nervous. I'm a licensed clinical psychologist based in Miami, Florida, and for more than 15 years I've walked alongside adults and adolescents through life's hardest moments.
